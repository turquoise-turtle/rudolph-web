Move this Mac's rules to another Mac

For when its user changes computers. Every rule in force here is copied to the new Mac as it is (policy, messages, expiry and collection of allowed executions); expired rules and rules being removed stay behind. A rule the new Mac already has exactly as here is left as it is, and one it has with a different policy, expiry, message, URL or CEL expression stops the move before anything changes.
