{{if .Editing}}

Editing is on. As a Rudolph.Admin you can add and remove global rules on the Rules page, rules for one Mac and its temporary client mode on that Mac's page (under Machines), and group rules on each group's page (under Groups). On the Fleet page you can set the fleet job's holiday mode, and on a list of ready Macs under Lockdown readiness add Macs to a Lockdown group. Macs get every change at their next sync.

Every change is recorded in Rudolph's audit trail with your name, as the CLI records its own: see the audit trail.
