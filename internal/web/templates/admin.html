Admin actions

{{if .Editing}}

Editing is on. As a Rudolph.Admin you can add and remove global rules on the Rules page, rules for one Mac and its temporary client mode on that Mac's page (under Machines), and group rules on each group's page (under Groups). Macs get every change at their next sync.

Every change is recorded in Rudolph's audit trail with your name, as the CLI records its own: see the audit trail.

{{else}}

This deployment is read-only. Editing is turned on with the deployment's enable_editing setting, which also grants the web view's role the writes it needs; the pages keep their copy-and-paste Rudolph CLI commands either way.

Changes made with the Rudolph CLI are recorded in Rudolph's audit trail with who made them: see the audit trail.

{{end}} {{end}}
